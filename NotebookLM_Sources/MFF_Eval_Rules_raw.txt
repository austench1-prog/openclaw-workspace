Understanding Evaluation Parameters at MyFunded Futures
Updated over 2 months ago

At MyFunded Futures, we believe in providing traders with a clear path to success. A significant part of this journey involves understanding the evaluation parameters that guide your trading experience. These parameters are not just arbitrary rules; they are designed based on years of market experience and are aimed at fostering disciplined and profitable trading habits.
​

 

Explanation of Parameters

Before delving into strategies and trades, it’s crucial to understand the parameters that will be used to assess your performance.

Profit Target: 

This is the predetermined profit amount you aim to achieve within the evaluation period. It serves as a benchmark for your trading success.

Maximum Drawdown: 

This is the maximum allowable loss from the peak of your account balance. It's a safety net, ensuring that traders don't risk too much capital.

Daily Loss Limit: 

This parameter sets a cap on how much you can lose in a single trading day. It's designed to prevent spiraling losses on particularly volatile days.

EOD (End of Day) Drawdown: 

This refers to the drawdown that is pegged to your positive account performance and is adjusted at the end of the trading day. If you increase your profit by $1.00, then your minimum account balance will also rise by $1.00. This mechanism ensures that traders are rewarded for positive performance by providing them with a cushion against potential future losses.

Intraday Drawdown (Rapid Plans): 

A trailing drawdown is a dynamic loss limit that increases as your account's equity reaches new highs, but does not decrease when losses occur. MyFunded Futures uses intraday drawdown to monitor a trader's risk exposure throughout the trading day. 

Consistency Rule: 

This rule ensures that traders don't swing wildly in their trading sizes or strategies. It promotes steady and consistent trading habits.

Minimum Trading Days: 

All evaluations require traders to trade for a minimum number of 2 days to ensure they are genuinely engaged and not just taking wild risks hoping for a lucky break.​

 

Start your Evaluation today

 

 

Importance and Impact on Trading

Understanding these parameters is not just about compliance; it's about internalizing the principles of disciplined trading:

Risk Management: 

The Maximum Drawdown and EOD Drawdown parameters are quintessential for instilling robust risk management habits. They ensure that traders don't get carried away by emotions and make impulsive decisions that can jeopardize their accounts.

Goal Orientation: 

The Profit Target parameter gives traders a clear goal to strive for. It encourages forward planning and strategic thinking.

Consistency: 

The Consistency Rule in our evaluation plans ensures that traders develop a steady trading rhythm, which is crucial for long-term success in the markets.
​

 

Managing Your Trading Accordingly

With a clear understanding of these parameters, here's how you can tailor your trading:

Plan Your Trades: Always have a clear entry and exit strategy. Know your stop-loss and take-profit levels before entering a trade.

Monitor Your Performance: Regularly check your performance against the evaluation parameters. 

Stay Informed: Keep abreast of market news and events. External factors can significantly impact the markets, and being informed will help you make better trading decisions.

Seek Feedback: Don't hesitate to reach out to the MFFU community or support team for feedback or clarifications on your trading or the evaluation parameters.
​

Mastering the MFFU Evaluation: Your Guide to Funded Trading

Evaluation Set Up For Success

Updated over a month ago

The MFFU funded trading program is a transformative system that builds disciplined, profitable traders. Each stage sharpens your ability to manage risk, maintain consistency, and adapt to dynamic market conditions. By progressing through the evaluation, you’ll develop the skills and confidence needed for long-term trading success, supported by MFFU’s robust framework and community resources.

 

 

The Three Stages of the MFFU Evaluation

 

1. Initial Evaluation: Demonstrate Your Trading Skills

Objective: Achieve profit targets on a virtual account while adhering to MFFU’s trading rules, proving your ability to trade effectively.

Duration: Flexible, with no fixed max number of trading days. Focus on meeting objectives at your own pace and in as little as 2 days. 

Key Focus: Emphasize risk management, consistent profitability, and compliance with rules like daily loss limits and approved strategies.

What to Expect: Trade in a simulated environment with a set account balance, designed to mirror real-world conditions. You’ll face realistic challenges to test your decision-making and discipline.

Outcome: Successful traders advance to the simulated funded stage, a significant step toward live trading.

 

 

2. Simulated Funded Stage: Prove Long-Term Consistency

Objective: Maintain profitability over an extended period, take payouts per MFFU’s payout policy, and demonstrate a repeatable trading strategy.

Duration: A longer phase to assess your performance across diverse market conditions, ensuring sustained success.

Key Focus: Blend profitability with disciplined risk management and a reliable trading plan that holds up under pressure.

What to Expect: Manage a simulated funded account, with the opportunity to take payouts while proving your strategy’s durability. This stage tests your ability to stay consistent in volatile markets – while earning.

Outcome: Traders who excel here progress to the live funded account, where real capital awaits.

 

 

3. Live Funded Account: Trade Real Markets

Objective: Trade with real capital in live markets, focusing on sustainable profits, account growth, and rule adherence.

Duration: Indefinite duration, continue trading as long as you remain profitable and compliant with MFFU’s guidelines.

Key Focus: Navigate real-world trading scenarios, manage emotions, and apply advanced strategies for long-term success and large payouts.

What to Expect: This is the pinnacle of the MFFU journey. You’ll trade live markets with real stakes, supported by MFFU’s tools and community. Expect dynamic conditions that test your resilience and adaptability.

Outcome: Live trading offers the chance to build a thriving career, with profits reflecting your skill and discipline.

 

 

What to Expect at Each Stage: Key Insights

Initial Evaluation: Success means proving your trading acumen through achievable profit targets and strict rule adherence. This stage builds the foundation for professional trading.

Simulated Funded Stage: Expect extended market scenarios to test your consistency. You’ll take payouts while refining a strategy that works across market cycles.

Live Funded Account: Trade live markets with confidence, leveraging skills honed in earlier stages. This is where your journey as a funded trader truly begins.

 

 

Strategies for Progressing Through the Evaluation

 

To move through the MFFU evaluation successfully, focus on these proven strategies:

Emphasize Consistency: Hitting profit targets is important, but sustained performance over time distinguishes top traders. Develop a strategy that delivers reliable results.

Adhere to Rules: Follow MFFU’s guidelines, such as consistency rules and approved trading methods, to avoid setbacks. Discipline is critical at every stage.

Commit to Growth: Engage with the MFFU community, attend webinars, and seek feedback from mentors to stay ahead of market trends and refine your approach.

Manage Emotions: Stay composed, especially in the live funded stage, where real capital is at stake. Practice mindfulness or journaling to maintain focus.

Analyze Your Trades: Regularly review your performance to identify strengths and weaknesses, ensuring continuous improvement.

 

To connect with like-minded traders, join the MFFU community on Discord for valuable insights and support.

 

 

 

Overcoming Common Challenges

Challenge: Difficulty maintaining consistent profits.

Solution: Prioritize high-probability trades and optimize your risk-reward ratio. Test strategies in the simulated stages to build confidence.

Challenge: Violating rules under market pressure.

Solution: Practice strict discipline early on, using the Initial Evaluation to ingrain habits that carry into live trading.

Challenge: Emotional stress in live markets.

Solution: Develop routines like trade journaling or meditation to stay calm and focused during high-stakes moments.

 

 

Benefits of the MFFU Evaluation Process

 

The MFFU evaluation is rigorous, but its rewards are substantial. By completing the program, you’ll gain:

Market-Ready Skills: Master risk management, strategy development, and emotional control to handle any market condition.

Professional Confidence: Simulated and live trading build the resilience needed for a sustainable career.

Community and Resources: MFFU’s active community, webinars, and mentorship opportunities provide ongoing support to fuel your growth.

Real-World Opportunities: The live funded account lets you trade with real capital, turning your skills into tangible financial rewards.

One Simply Evaluation for All Plans! One Simple Consistency Rule!

Updated over 2 months ago

MyFunded Futures has adopted a streamlined approach to evaluations. Traders can now follow a standardized evaluation process on their path to simulated funding and live account trading. The evaluation rules remain consistent, regardless of the account type you choose. 
​
​
​What is the evaluation consistency rule and how it work? 

 

The consistency rule is 50% for all MyFunded Futures evaluations on the Rapid, Flex & Pro plans. (Pro Plan One day pass has no consistency rule)
​
This consistency rule means no single day's profit should exceed 50% of your total evaluation profits made. This rule promotes discipline and consistent trading habits which highlights a prop firm's commitment to encourage responsible trading. 
​
​
​Example on how the 50% consistency rule works:

Total Profit Target on a 50k account: $3,000

Max Profit a Trader should achieve: $1,500

Calculation is: Total profit target / 2 = Max Daily Profit


​Important note: Exceeding the 50% consistency target does not breach the account. Traders who achieve more than 50% of their total profit target in one day simply need to trade additional days until consistency is met. 
​
Traders may utilize the consistency calculator link below to determine profit target. 
​https://www.consistencycalculator.com

Understanding the Difference Between Account Resets and Renewals

Resets vs. Renewals

Updated over 2 months ago

Managing your trading account effectively requires a clear understanding of the options available. Two common actions, resetting an account and renewing an account, are distinct features. Misunderstanding their differences can lead to confusion.
​
Here's what you need to know:
​
​What is an Account Reset?
* An account reset is a manual action you take to restore your trading account to its    original state, whether it's breached or not. This option is ideal if you are seeking a fresh start without waiting for the next billing cycle.
​
​When you reset an account:
​* Your account balance is restored to its initial balance.

* Your progress starts over, including any gains or losses accumulated. 
​

Key Point: A reset does not extend the life of your evaluation account. It restores its condition while keeping the same date tied to your current billing cycle. 

 

Looking to get Funded with MFFU? Start here.

 


​What is an Account Renewal?

 

A renewal happens automatically every 30 days unless you cancel your account before the next billing date. Renewal ensures uninterrupted access to your evaluation account, allowing you to continue trading. 
​
​When your account renews:

Your subscription is extended for another 30 days.

Your evaluation progress remains intact, if the account is not in breached status. Gains and losses carry over. 

If the evaluation account is in breached status at the time of the billing, it will be automatically reactivated and its balance will be restored to its initial balance.

 

Key Point: Renewal does not reset your evaluation account, unless your account is breached at the time of rebilling. Additionally, you're provided with a reset credit if your evaluation account is not breached at the time of the rebilling. (First month only).

It's crucial to understand the differences between resetting and renewing your account to effectively manage your trading journey.
